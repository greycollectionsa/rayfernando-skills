# AI-search readiness

Use this reference when the task mentions Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Bing/Copilot, GEO, AEO, crawler controls, llms.txt, AI citations, or “optimising for AI”.

## Durable default

Start with normal SEO fundamentals:

- crawlable, indexable public pages.
- clear entity and service information.
- useful original content.
- visible evidence.
- good internal architecture.
- accurate structured data.
- trustworthy, consistent business details.
- fast, usable pages.
- first-party measurement.

Do not replace these with speculative AI-only tactics.

## Google AI features

Current Google guidance says the foundational SEO practices used for Search remain relevant for AI search experiences. Do not claim a special AI schema, AI text file, or separate technical recipe is required for Google AI features.

Focus on:

- index eligibility.
- snippet eligibility.
- people-first useful content.
- clear facts and entities.
- structured data that matches the page.
- strong internal linking.
- original value and evidence.
- Search Console measurement.

Avoid “GEO hacks” such as hidden AI copy, fake references, mass inauthentic mentions, or unnecessary files presented as ranking requirements.

## ChatGPT search

When a business wants public pages to be discoverable in ChatGPT search:

- review `robots.txt`.
- review CDN/WAF/bot mitigation.
- consider access for `OAI-SearchBot`.
- remember `GPTBot` is a separate training-related control.
- never expose private routes simply to improve discoverability.
- use `noindex`/access controls appropriately for content that should not appear publicly.
- measure referral traffic and qualified outcomes where possible.

## Bing / Copilot

Use durable Bing fundamentals:

- discoverable crawlable pages.
- sitemaps and/or IndexNow where useful.
- descriptive page structure.
- accurate business/entity information.
- quality and trust signals.
- current content.
- internal/external links that help discovery.

Do not promise that IndexNow, schema, or any crawler submission creates rankings or Copilot citations.

## llms.txt

Treat `llms.txt` as optional experimentation.

Do not describe it as:

- required for Google AI features.
- required for ChatGPT search.
- a ranking factor.
- a substitute for robots.txt/sitemaps/indexable content.
- a security mechanism.

If a site already uses it, keep it accurate, public-only, useful, and subordinate to real website content.

## AI visibility measurement

AI answers are probabilistic and change over time. Use a repeatable measurement set:

- stable prompts/questions.
- engine and date.
- whether the business is mentioned.
- whether the answer is materially correct.
- cited/linked sources.
- competitor mentions where relevant.
- referral traffic.
- qualified conversion.
- material changes made between measurement periods.

Do not infer “AI market share” from a single screenshot or one answer.
